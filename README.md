# SQL-Project-1
**Project Title: Retail Sales Analysis Using SQL**
📌 Description:
This project demonstrates my ability to perform structured data analysis using SQL on a retail sales dataset. It includes a series of queries designed to extract meaningful business insights, identify customer behavior patterns, and support strategic decision-making. The dataset contains transactional data such as customer demographics, product categories, sales amounts, and timestamps.
The project is built using MySQL and covers key analytical tasks such as filtering, aggregation, ranking, and time-based analysis. It’s designed to help businesses understand performance across categories, customer segments, and time periods.

**Key Business Questions & Insights**
Q1: Sales on a specific date
Identifies daily performance and helps track promotional impact or seasonal spikes.
Q2: High-volume clothing sales in Nov-2022
Reveals demand surges in specific categories, useful for inventory planning.
Q3: Total sales by category
Highlights top-performing product lines, guiding marketing and stocking decisions.
Q4: Average age of Beauty category buyers
Helps tailor campaigns based on customer demographics.
Q5: Transactions with high total sale (>1000)
Flags premium purchases for loyalty targeting or upselling opportunities.
Q6: Transactions by gender and category
Supports gender-based segmentation and personalized marketing.
Q7: Monthly average sales & best-selling months
Enables seasonality analysis and revenue forecasting.
Q8: Top 5 customers by total sales
Identifies high-value customers for retention strategies.
Q9: Unique customers per category
Measures category reach and customer diversity.
Q10: Orders by shift (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)
Reveals peak shopping hours, useful for staffing and promotions.

**Business Impact:**
- Customer Insights: Helps understand who buys what and when, enabling targeted marketing.
- Category Performance: Guides product strategy by identifying strong and weak performers.
- Time-Based Trends: Supports forecasting and seasonal campaign planning.
- Operational Efficiency: Shift analysis informs staffing and resource allocation.
- Revenue Optimization: Identifies high-value customers and transactions for strategic focus.

 **Technologies Used:**
- SQL (MySQL)
- Relational Database Concepts
- Aggregation, Filtering, Window Functions
- Date & Time Functions
